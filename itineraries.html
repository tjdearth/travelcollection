Experience Morocco 10 Days

Imperial Cities & Sahara

Culture

From the bustling souks of Marrakech to the silence of the Sahara, a journey through Morocco's imperial heart.

Day 1
Arrive in Casablanca Meet your private driver at the airport and transfer to your riad. Evening welcome dinner at a rooftop restaurant overlooking the Hassan II Mosque.
Days 2–3
Explore the Fez Medina Two full days in the world's largest car-free urban area. Visit tanneries, medieval madrasas, and artisan workshops with a local historian guide.
Day 4
Scenic Drive Through the Middle Atlas Wind through cedar forests and Berber villages en route to the Sahara. Stop at Ifrane and the Ziz Valley gorges.
Days 5–6
Sahara Desert Experience Camel trek to a luxury desert camp among the Erg Chebbi dunes. Stargazing, Berber drumming, and a sunrise over endless sand.
Days 7–8
Marrakech Immersion Private guided tour of the souks, Bahia Palace, and Jardin Majorelle. Rooftop cooking class and an evening in Jemaa el-Fna square.
Day 9
Atlas Mountains Day Trip Drive to the Ourika Valley for a hike to waterfalls, lunch at a Berber home, and panoramic views of the High Atlas peaks.
Day 10
Depart Marrakech Farewell breakfast and private transfer to the airport.

Siena & San Gimignano Explore Siena's Piazza del Campo and cathedral, then drive to the medieval towers of San Gimignano for gelato and sunset views.

Oshinobi Travel 12 Days

Tokyo to Kyoto Discovery

Culture

Ancient temples, neon cities, and everything in between — Japan at its most immersive.

Days 1–2
Tokyo Exploration Discover the contrast of Shibuya's neon crossings and Asakusa's ancient Senso-ji temple. Tsukiji outer market food tour and a private sushi omakase dinner.
Day 3
Day Trip to Nikko Visit the ornate Toshogu Shrine, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, surrounded by towering cedar forests and sacred bridges.

Hakone & Mount Fuji Cruise Lake Ashi with Mount Fuji views, ride the Hakone Ropeway, and soak in a traditional onsen hot spring.
Day 5
Bullet Train to Takayama Experience the Shinkansen, then explore Takayama's beautifully preserved Edo-era streets and morning markets.
Day 6
Shirakawa-go Village Visit the UNESCO thatched-roof farmhouses in this remote mountain valley. Lunch with a local family.
Days 7–8
Kanazawa Explore Kenroku-en (one of Japan's top three gardens), the samurai district, Omi-cho market, and a gold-leaf workshop experience.
Days 9–10
Kyoto Temples & Traditions Private guided visits to Kinkaku-ji, Fushimi Inari, and the bamboo grove. Evening walk through the geisha district of Gion with a tea ceremony.
Day 11
Nara Day Trip Feed the sacred deer in Nara Park and explore Todai-ji temple, home to the world's largest bronze Buddha.
Day 12
Depart from Osaka Morning visit to Osaka's Dotonbori food street, then transfer to Kansai Airport.

Culture · Food & Wine

Emerald-green coffee hills, colonial streets, and Caribbean warmth.

Fly to Cartagena Arrive in the Caribbean gem. Settle into your boutique hotel in the walled old city. Sunset drinks on the city walls.
Day 7
Cartagena & Rosario Islands Morning walking tour of the old city's balconied streets, churches, and plazas. Afternoon speedboat to the Rosario Islands for snorkeling and a seafood lunch.
Day 8
Depart Cartagena Final morning coffee in Plaza Santo Domingo and transfer to the airport.
